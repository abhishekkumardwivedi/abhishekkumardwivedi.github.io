Safety-critical engineering needs several complementary lenses. These articles separate fault-free functional limitations, malfunctioning behaviour, and learning-enabled-system risks while showing how their evidence eventually meets at vehicle level.

## Current series

1. [SOTIF in Practice: Finding the Unsafe Without a Fault](/articles/sotif-autonomous-driving/) — triggering conditions, functional insufficiencies, foreseeable misuse, and residual risk.
2. [Functional Safety in Practice: From Hazard to Fault-Tolerant Control](/articles/functional-safety-av/) — ISO 26262 thinking across hazards, safety goals, architecture, diagnostics, degraded operation, and release evidence.
3. [AI Safety in the Vehicle: From Dataset to Runtime Guardrails](/articles/automotive-ai-safety/) — data, training, verification, deployment, monitoring, and controlled model change.

- ISO 21434 and UN R155/R156 cybersecurity and update governance
