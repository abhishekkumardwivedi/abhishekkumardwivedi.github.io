title: Algorithms & Problem Solving
description: Reusable algorithm patterns, data structures, complexity reasoning, and concise notes from solved problems.
---

This section will turn individual coding exercises into reusable engineering knowledge. The emphasis will be on recognising a pattern, deriving the solution, proving why it works, understanding complexity, and recording the mistakes that commonly lead to incorrect or slow implementations.

## Planned collections

- Arrays, strings, hashing, sorting, and two-pointer techniques
- Binary search and answer-space search
- Greedy methods and interval reasoning
- Trees, graphs, traversal, shortest paths, and disjoint sets
- Dynamic programming and state design
- Heaps, queues, stacks, and monotonic structures
- Bit manipulation, number theory, and combinatorics
- C++ correctness, complexity, and implementation notes

Articles will be grouped by technique rather than by problem number, making the library useful for revision and interview preparation.
