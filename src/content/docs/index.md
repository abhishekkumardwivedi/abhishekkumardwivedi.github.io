## A career built across layers

I started close to silicon—firmware, ARM systems, bootloaders, board bring-up, and hardware debugging—and progressively moved through Embedded Linux, Android/AOSP, BSP, middleware, security, connectivity, and complete product platforms. Today, I shape software-defined vehicle and digital-cockpit systems while continuing to build in edge AI, autonomous-vehicle software-in-the-loop, connected telemetry, and AI-native systems engineering.

## Engineering with a product perspective

My Master’s from **King’s College London** changed how I frame technical work. I consider not only whether a system can be engineered, but which problem deserves solving, how value will be created, how a product should evolve, and what organisation and delivery model will make it sustainable. **Agile Leadership at Stanford Graduate School of Business** further strengthened how I lead change and align multidisciplinary teams.

That combination—deep platform engineering, system thinking, product ownership, and entrepreneurial perspective—defines the kind of work I seek and the way I lead it.
