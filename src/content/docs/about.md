I am a product architect and embedded-platform engineer with more than 19 years of experience building systems across automotive, consumer electronics, IoT, Android, and Linux.

My work has consistently sat at system boundaries: application to framework, framework to HAL, operating system to BSP, software to hardware, and engineering execution to product strategy. I enjoy making those boundaries understandable and turning incomplete, cross-domain problems into coherent architectures and deliverable plans.

## Current focus

- Software-defined vehicle and digital-cockpit architecture
- Android Automotive, Embedded Linux, QNX-hosted systems, and Qualcomm platforms
- Edge AI, perception, heterogeneous compute, and autonomous-system experimentation
- Platform security, OTA, diagnostics, and connected systems
- AI-native systems-engineering tools and product innovation

## Beyond engineering

I hold a Master’s in Strategic Entrepreneurship & Innovation from King’s College London and completed Agile Leadership at Stanford Graduate School of Business. These experiences strengthened how I connect engineering decisions with customer value, organisational design, and long-term product strategy.

Outside work, I enjoy learning, writing, prototyping, fitness, travel, and exploring how technology can make complex physical systems safer and more useful.
