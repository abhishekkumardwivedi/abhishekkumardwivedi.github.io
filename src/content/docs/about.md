I am a product architect and engineering leader with around two decades of experience building complex embedded and connected systems. My work spans automotive platforms, Android and Embedded Linux, BSP and middleware, hardware–software integration, platform security, edge AI, and product strategy.

I have grown from writing low-level firmware and platform software to shaping complete system concepts, technical roadmaps, interfaces, and delivery models. That journey gives me a practical architectural perspective: I can move from a product goal to system decomposition, and then follow a problem through the operating system, middleware, drivers, hardware interfaces, security boundaries, and application behaviour.

## My engineering journey

I began close to the hardware, working with ARM-based systems, bootloaders, board bring-up, firmware, and debugging tools. I later moved deeper into Linux and Android—telephony, audio, framework and native services, HALs, kernels, virtualization, secure boot, trusted execution, and platform integration.

Across consumer, IoT, and automotive programmes, I have helped take products from concept and early prototypes to production-quality platforms. My experience includes connected devices and gateways, wireless sensing, camera and media pipelines, secure device identity, OTA, diagnostics, and cross-layer root-cause analysis. I am comfortable reading schematics, understanding PCB and mechanical constraints, and working with hardware teams when a software symptom originates below the software boundary.

Today, much of my work centres on software-defined vehicles and digital cockpits: Android Automotive, Embedded Linux and QNX-based systems, Qualcomm automotive platforms, vehicle interfaces, connectivity, security, diagnostics, and coordination across OEM, semiconductor, supplier, and distributed engineering teams.

More recently, I have been working on autonomous-vehicle software-in-the-loop architecture, edge-inference deployment, and an AI-native systems-engineering platform for modelling automotive hardware, software, communication, deployment, and traceability.

These projects are not isolated technology demonstrations. They help me understand how architecture decisions behave when converted into code, data flows, deployment constraints, and usable engineering tools.

## How I approach systems

- **Start with the whole product.** Understand the user, operating environment, safety and security concerns, lifecycle, and business objective before optimising a component.
- **Make boundaries explicit.** Clear ownership, interfaces, timing, data contracts, failure behaviour, and traceability prevent many late integration problems.
- **Stay technically grounded.** Architecture is stronger when it remains connected to code, logs, hardware behaviour, performance measurements, and implementation trade-offs.
- **Design for change.** Platforms should accommodate new hardware, software capabilities, regulations, and product variants without requiring a complete redesign.
- **Build alignment.** Complex products succeed when specialists across hardware, software, safety, security, product, and suppliers share the same system picture.
